        Here’s how one lead moves through the system, start to finish.

        01
        01

        Capture. Every lead lands in one place.

        Calls, missed calls, website forms, texts, WhatsApp, Facebook — today they scatter across phones, inboxes, and sticky notes. The system catches every one in a single queue the moment it arrives, and records where it came from and whether you have permission to text or email back.

        What this replaces: “Did anyone see that form from Tuesday?”

        02
        02

        Respond. In seconds, every time.

        The moment a lead arrives, the system answers — a text back to a missed call, an instant reply to a form, at 2 pm or 9 pm. It sounds human, it uses your words, and it opens the conversation while your competitor's voicemail is still talking.

        What this replaces: the voicemail nobody checks until Thursday.

        03
        03

        Qualify. A few useful questions.

        The system asks what you'd ask: what do they need, where are they, when do they want it. Answers are scored against rules built for your trade — so a real prospect in your territory is treated differently than a price-shopper three counties away.

        What this replaces: driving forty minutes to quote a job that was never real.

        04
        04

        Route. A named owner, every time.

        Every qualified lead is assigned to a specific person on your team — not a shared inbox, not "whoever sees it first." If it sits untouched too long, the system notices and raises a flag before the lead goes cold.

        What this replaces: “I thought you were handling that one.”

        05
        05

        Book. A real slot on a real calendar.

        Good leads don't get "we'll be in touch." They get offered an actual time — a call, a visit, an estimate — on your team's live calendar. Confirmations and reminders go out automatically so booked doesn't quietly turn into no-show.

        What this replaces: three days of phone tag to schedule one estimate.

        06
        06

        Follow up. Politely. Relentlessly.

        Most jobs aren't lost on the first call — they're lost in the silence after the quote. The system follows up on every open conversation and every sent quote until there's an answer: booked, won, lost, or "not now." Leads that go quiet get parked, then re-approached when the timing makes sense. And when a job wraps, it asks the happy customer for the review.

        What this replaces: the quote you meant to chase and never did.

        07
        07

        Track. One board, the whole truth.

        Every lead sits at a named stage — from New to Won, Lost, or Nurture — on one board you can read in ten seconds. What's waiting on a quote. What's stalled. What closed this week. No more reconstructing reality from three inboxes and someone's memory.

        What this replaces: finding out a lead died three weeks after it happened.

        08
        08

        Improve. One leak at a time, every month.

        Once a month you get a plain-language report: where leads came in, where they dropped, and the single biggest leak in the pipe. We fix that one, measure it, then move to the next. The system also watches itself — if anything stops working, we know before you do.

        What this replaces: “marketing feels like it's working… I think?”
