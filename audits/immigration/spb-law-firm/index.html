03 —

Infrastructure Fixes

Actionable Protocol

These structural changes are the highest-leverage path to page 1. Most can be implemented within 30 days.

A

Build visa-type landing pages for business immigration

H-1B, L-1, O-1, EB-1, EB-2 NIW, and PERM labor certification each need dedicated pages. The firm's IT/software focus means these employer-sponsored visa pages would target a high-value, underserved niche in DFW.

B

Create content for individual immigration services

Family-based petitions, naturalization, asylum, and DACA need their own pages. Even if business immigration is the primary focus, family immigration content captures the broader DFW search volume.

C

Implement Attorney schema and firm-level LegalService schema

Three attorneys with distinct specializations need individual schema entries. The firm's Lewisville location and multi-practice structure should be fully represented in structured data.
