03 —

Infrastructure Fixes

Actionable Protocol

These structural changes are the highest-leverage path to page 1. Most can be implemented within 30 days.

A

Build practice area pages for criminal defense and personal injury in Palm Harbor

Separate pages for "criminal defense Palm Harbor," "DUI attorney Pinellas County," and "car accident lawyer Palm Harbor" are the direct path to ranking — the firm's homepage alone cannot compete.

B

Port 48 Birdeye reviews to the firm's own Google Business Profile

Third-party review platforms don't build the firm's domain authority. Funneling reviews to Google and embedding them on the site converts a trust asset into a ranking asset.

C

Add Attorney schema to all attorney pages

Koulianos and Jocic both have established credentials — Attorney schema with bar admission dates, practice areas, and geo-coordinates makes the firm eligible for Google's lawyer knowledge panels.
