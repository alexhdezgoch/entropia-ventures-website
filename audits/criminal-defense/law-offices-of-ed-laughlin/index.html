03 —

Infrastructure Fixes

Actionable Protocol

These structural changes are the highest-leverage path to page 1. Most can be implemented within 30 days.

A

Rebuild Site on Modern CMS with Practice Pages

The PHP single-page site cannot be optimized. A WordPress rebuild with separate pages for Bankruptcy, Criminal Defense, Personal Injury, and Divorce — each with geo-specific copy for Waco, Temple, and Killeen — is the prerequisite for all other improvements.

B

Add Schema + Google Business Profile

With 3 office locations and no structured data, the firm is absent from every local pack query. LegalService schema + GBP optimization for each location would produce quick visibility wins even before blog content is live.

C

Convert Radio Show into Blog Content

Ed already produces legal education content weekly on News Radio 1400. Transcribing or summarizing Law Talk Live segments into blog posts would create a fast content library targeting Waco search terms with zero extra production effort.
