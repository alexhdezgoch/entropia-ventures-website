Infrastructure Fixes

Actionable Protocol

These structural changes are the highest-leverage path to page 1. Most can be implemented within 30 days.

A

Fix indexation — submit sitemap to Google Search Console and resolve any noindex tags

A site with zero indexed pages is functionally nonexistent online. The first priority is diagnosing why Google isn't indexing the domain (robots.txt block, noindex tag, or crawl errors) and correcting it immediately.

B

Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile for Saint Petersburg criminal defense

With no organic ranking presence, GBP is the fastest path to local visibility. A complete profile with practice areas, photos, and consistent NAP data can surface in the map pack within weeks — often before the website itself ranks.

C

Add LegalService schema and build separate Criminal Defense and Family Law practice pages

Rebecca Hamilton handles both criminal defense and family law — two distinct client journeys requiring separate optimized pages. Each page with proper schema gives Google the signal it needs to route the right searcher to the right service.
