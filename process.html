How It Works

From audit to installed.

Four stages, start to finish. No long onboarding, no black box — you see exactly what we build and why.

Four stages. No black box.

Here is exactly what happens, from the first look at your business to the monthly loop that keeps making it better.

01

Audit

Free, from the outside, delivered in 48 hours.

call

We test your lines

We call your main number, fill your forms, and message your channels — like a real customer would.

timer

We time everything

How long until a real answer? Minutes, hours, or never — we measure it.

alt_route

We map your lead sources

Google, ads, referrals, social, phone — where leads come from and where each one goes.

description

You get the leak report

A plain-language breakdown of where leads die today and what that costs you. Yours to keep either way.

02

Install

Days, not months. Your team keeps working while we build around them.

hub

One place for every lead

Every call, form, and message lands in one system — tagged with its source, with consent recorded from the start.

bolt

Instant response

Within seconds, every new lead gets a useful, human-sounding reply on an allowed channel — text, WhatsApp, or email.

checklist

Smart qualification

The system asks a few useful questions — what they need, where they are, when — and scores the lead.

person_pin_circle

A named owner

Qualified leads route to a specific person on your team. Nothing sits in a shared inbox hoping someone notices.

event_available

Real bookings

Good leads get a real slot on a real calendar — a call or a site visit — respecting your hours and territory.

sync_alt

Your CRM or ours

We install and run a proven stack for you, or connect to your Salesforce or HubSpot. No migration, no retraining.

03

Run

The system works every lead until it's won, lost, or parked — and tells us when something stalls.

forum

Follow-up that doesn't quit

Polite, persistent sequences run until the lead books, buys, or says no. It never gets busy and never forgets.

view_kanban

Every deal on one board

Appointments, quotes, and deals move through fixed stages — you always know what's open and what's stuck.

notifications_active

Stall alerts

A quote with no follow-up, a lead with no owner, a no-show with no reschedule — the system flags it before it dies.

monitor_heart

System health, watched

Failed messages, opt-outs, and errors are caught and handled. We watch the machine so you don't have to.

The stages every lead moves through

New → Contacted → Engaged → Qualified → Booked → Appointment → Quote sent → Won / Lost / Nurture
04

Improve

A monthly loop that finds the biggest leak and fixes one thing at a time.

insights

The monthly report

What came in by source, what booked, what closed, and where the biggest leak is — in plain language, not a dashboard you can't read.

science

One change at a time

We test a single improvement each cycle and measure what it did. No guessing, no ten-changes-at-once chaos.

history

Old leads, revived

Past leads that went quiet get a reactivation pass — often the cheapest revenue you'll find.

reviews

Wins turn into proof

Won jobs trigger review requests, so every good customer makes the next one easier.

Research first

Radical transparency

Direct access

You talk to the person doing the work. No account managers, no relay chain.

No lock-in

Your data and your tools stay yours. Leave whenever you want, with everything you came in with.
